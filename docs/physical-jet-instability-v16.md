# v16 finite-width jet response
Separate experimental branch, preserves v15 exact graph/solver for regression. New graphs enable a distributed convection delay. The prior centered jet favored the fifth resonance: with delay near 0.48 ms, both the quarter-period and five-quarter-period feedback phase conditions can be met. A single delayed sample with frequency-independent growth exaggerates short-wavelength coherent deflection.
The new finite-width receptivity approximation uses a seven-tap positive binomial quadrature of convective transit times, centered at W/(0.4U); its normalized coefficients sum to one. Relative delay spread depends on jet thickness/travel, viscosity through sqrt(2 nu tau), and geometric spread. It is an empirical reduced transport kernel, not an Orr–Sommerfeld eigenvalue solution or an exact measured jet response. Its spectral attenuation acts on the acoustic disturbance BEFORE jet splitting and pressure work; no output filter, frequency clamp, or pitch-specific exciter. The original energy ledger and jet kinetic work cap are unchanged.
