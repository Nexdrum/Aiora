# Universal physical construction v12 — stage 1
Design contract: energy -> transfer object -> interaction -> object -> optional further objects/interactions -> output. Material identity and geometry identity must never select a sound algorithm. Presets are editable values only. Fixed reference environment: 20 C, 101325 Pa, 9.80665 m/s2, dry ambient air 1.204 kg/m3, 343 m/s.
Stage 1: per-object editable density, hardness, elastic recovery, shear modulus, bulk modulus (inverse compressibility), internal loss, viscosity, order, alignment, scale, porosity and roughness. Density, shear, bulk, viscosity and damping use logarithmic mappings. Effective density and stiffness include porosity; isotropic E derived from K and G before empirical anisotropy and the fluid limit. Order/scale use an empirical frequency-dependent scattering proxy. Roughness and recovery affect moving contact. Viscosity affects pressure transmission and modal losses. Material IDs remain only for legacy compatibility. No material dropdown in new graphs. Initial material values are editable.
Reduced solver: geometry aspect ratios and the near-zero static shear limit choose the existing modal families; a soft nearly incompressible solid remains solid. This is not a full viscoelastic continuum solver. Existing geometry sliders remain. Fluid geometry currently uses cavity approximation. Reference environment constants are declared; gravity and dynamic ambient effects are not yet simulated. Surface tension, placed openings, multiple cavities, arbitrary occupancy, free stream geometry, reciprocal coupling, full output placement, and material/geometry tabs are later stages. No claim of physical validation. APT, V/M/B and old regression paths retained.

## Integrated prototype stage
The object editor now has five navigable sections (tap EDIT SECTION): Material, Geometry, Cavities, Openings, Fluid form. Each object has two fixed pocket slots and three fixed hole slots, disabled by zero length/size. Cross-section edge count and rounding, wall fill, wall thickness, pocket position/extent/offset and opening position/azimuth/aspect/neck influence reduced volume or acoustic model. Ambient-air cavity modes occupy two of the seven modal slots; they use a reduced Helmholtz/closed pressure estimate. Cavities are approximated by ellipsoidal fractions, not boolean mesh intersections; openings preferentially couple by axial proximity. No arbitrary 3D mesh, collision or multi-material region solver. Fluid form has confinement, spread, direction and fill, with gravity/capillary free-surface terms and confinement-dependent pressure loss. Added surface tension. Pressure flow remains reduced, not CFD. Connection sliders add sender/receiver transverse and depth coordinates and elevation; output links expose position, area, angle and distance. Receiver reaction includes bounded reciprocal modal-velocity mixing for adhesive contacts; this is not a fully reciprocal energy-conserving network. No new presets. Geometry slots and schematic are bounded and slider-driven. The existing B calibration still tracks the primary structural/pressure frequency, not all secondary cavity modes. Tests are newly added and require CI verification.
Boundary cases: the new construction controls permit zero contact/reception aperture and zero axial tension. An untensioned slender solid uses a bending contribution rather than requiring artificial tension. Polygon area is a reduced inscribed-ellipse estimate. Legacy parameter bounds and B assignments remain unchanged; new feature coordinates are not yet individually B-assignable.

## Live audition correction
The editor and keyboard can send an explicit PhysicalNoteOn event containing the current graph snapshot and V/M values, independent of operator patch and separately queued binding state. Draw frames and transport starts synchronize binding; full queue does not mark an unsent binding clean. Constructed preview uses a dry independent mix path with fixed 16x instrument-level calibration (transport uses the same calibration before track FX); no auto-gain, hidden oscillator or synthesized fallback. The lab can audition even while a drum track is selected; transport remains melodic-track-only and session-only. This fixes routing and improves nominal level, but does not establish device playback verification. Existing operator/pad previews remain on their original path.

## Guitar example and contact stability
GUITAR loads a five-node graph (energy, pick, steel-like string, soft-tissue palm, output). No instrument-specific DSP or hidden preset. The string branches to the palm and directly to pickup. M on the palm interaction travels from axial endpoint 0 to midpoint .5; overlap is sin²(pi x) for M-assigned constructed contacts. Stationary contact loss acts on source modal state and depends on softness, material loss, restitution, area and contact position. The previous delayed receiver-to-own-input injection and one-sided modal copying are removed for construction graphs. Fluid contact no longer applies a square-root small-signal gain. Forward flux is bounded by input plus decaying contact state. This deliberately replaces the unsafe reciprocal approximation with passive loading; full reciprocal conservative coupling remains unresolved. Tests compare the exact same graph against the prior commit and log attack, post-M, late energy, peak and final stored energy. Guitar uses fixed physical dimensions/tension; pitch calibration remains manually available. No special waveform or gain for the guitar.
