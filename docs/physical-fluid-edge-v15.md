# v15 powered fluid-edge interaction
Isolated experimental branch. v14 and production untouched. New graph version flag only selects integrator capability; there is no flute waveform or instrument identifier. A free fluid transfer object (first driven object), a solid edge, and a connected confined fluid volume form a jet/window interaction when the jet-to-edge distance is nonzero. The JET EDGE button replaces the generic EXAMPLE button, preserving guitar, bell and drum. New link controls: jet travel 0–30 mm and bevel 5–90 degrees; existing receiver position/M sets edge offset, motion angle and elevation set incidence, jet width/depth/spread/flow angle and edge rounding determine profile. Existing window link controls area and engagement.
The model approximates shear-layer convection at 0.4 U, growth exp(0.3 W/h), delayed acoustic cross-flow deflection, a Bickley profile split tanh((eta-y0)/b), and its pressure-source derivative. Based on Auvray et al., JASA 136, 389–400 (2014), and https://euphonics.org/11-8-1-the-jet-drive-model-for-a-recorder-or-flute/. Angle, bevel, spread and sharpness factors are reduced empirical geometry terms. A small broadband disturbance seeds oscillation and is charged to jet kinetic energy. No pitch oscillator. Open fluid acoustic ports use cosine velocity mode shapes.
Through-flow kinetic energy flux is 0.5*rho*A*U^3; per step capped by stored jet kinetic energy. Pressure applied to the tube's modal port does exact impulse work; positive work capped by available jet energy, negative work and unused energy logged as exhaust/contact loss. Bernoulli pressure loss is a passive impulse. JetWork and JetExhaust are diagnostic subsets, not additional terms in the energy balance. Edge is treated as a rigid splitter for this interaction, not an independently vibrating fluid-structure boundary. Multiple windows divide throughput; max four. Delay history stores a signal describing jet deflection, not an independent energy reservoir. No full CFD, vortex solver, calibrated mouth radiation, lip mechanics, or guaranteed acoustic pitch. Tests export actual WAV/CSV, check balance, silence, release, geometry and multi-rate output; a DFT reports dominant frequency and spectral concentration. v14 examples remain on same energy-conserving path.
