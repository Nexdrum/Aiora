# Energy-conserving physical solver v13 (experimental)
Branch intentionally separate from v12 and production. The v12 controls, graph model and legacy solver remain. New editor graphs opt into the SI integrator. Guitar loads the same editable construction; bell/drum/pressure-duct/friction-string are regression configurations, not hidden synthesis modes.

## Model
Each vibrating object stores generalized modal displacement (m), velocity (m/s), effective mass (kg) and angular frequency. The transfer object also has a rigid drive degree of freedom. APT applies an impulse to the transfer mass, with work computed exactly as J*v+J²/(2m) and bounded by finite 22 microjoule normalized budget or sustained 6 mW normalized power. The normalization of Potential is retained from v12; it is not an independent physical unit slider.

Free modes undergo an exact harmonic rotation followed by passive attenuation. Contact stiffness is effective modulus times contact cross-section divided by deformation length; critical damping uses generalized inverse mass. For relative port velocity u and spring extension s, midpoint impulse J = dt*[k*s+(c+k*dt/2)*u]/[1+dt*A*(c+k*dt/2)/2], with A the combined inverse effective port mass. Equal/opposite impulses update both generalized momenta. The viscous loss is c*dt*u_mid². Unilateral separation discards unresolved residual spring energy as a recorded loss, never amplifies it. A moving contact's increased spring stiffness accounts for explicit control work; decreased stiffness is recorded as lost energy. Friction uses a dissipative capped velocity-weakening impulse; it is not a full stick-slip bow model. Fluid pressure is a reduced compliant mechanical port, not a jet/reed/CFD model. Geometry, position, overlap and material coefficients determine the port. No per-mode amplitude limiter or normalized audio transfer.

Radiation uses an ambient-air impedance-based dashpot with approximate radiation efficiency ka. Reception pressure is an approximation Z_air*velocity*efficiency*area/orientation/distance. Only output divides pressure by a fixed 2 Pa full-scale; final audio mixer retains its tanh limiter. No 16x v12 calibration for SI graphs. Energy ledger in joules separately records source work, positive work budget, external tuning/contact control work, material loss, contact loss, radiation and stored modal/contact energy. Source-off passive energy must not rise; tests check balance residual independently.

## Limits
Modal bases, effective masses, material loss and contact areas are reduced approximations; solid deformation is not fully 3D, geometry is not a mesh, cavity modes are approximate, fluid flow lacks turbulence and self-oscillation, and violin bow friction is an initial velocity-weakening model, not validated stick-slip. Radiation pressure is not a calibrated microphone response. Y sets first-contact arrival, not a bidirectional propagation delay after engagement. Pitch B preserves generalized momentum and displacement, accounting for its external work. Gravity/capillarity remain in reduced fluid frequency model. Device performance and acoustic realism are not established by energy conservation. Test artifact contains mono WAV and CSV energy/displacement/pressure traces; audio is fixed-scale, not peak-normalized. Existing v12 tests are still run.
